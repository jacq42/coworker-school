# Coworker School Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Educational Quality First
Every generated learning material MUST be pedagogically sound and age-appropriate.
Content must follow established educational best practices and learning theory.
All materials require clear learning objectives, structured progression, and assessment criteria.
No content shall be generated without explicit educational context and target learning outcomes.

**Rationale**: Educational integrity is non-negotiable - AI-generated content must serve genuine learning purposes and maintain high academic standards.

### II. Multilingual and Multicultural Awareness
All agents MUST respect linguistic diversity and cultural contexts in learning materials.
German language exercises shall follow current spelling and grammar standards (neue deutsche Rechtschreibung).
Cross-cultural sensitivity is required when generating examples and scenarios.
Content must be inclusive and avoid cultural bias or stereotypes.

**Rationale**: Education serves diverse learners - AI systems must reflect and respect this diversity while maintaining cultural sensitivity.

### III. Structured Learning Pathways (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Every learning plan MUST follow a logical progression from basic to advanced concepts.
Prerequisites must be clearly defined and validated before advanced topics.
Learning tasks shall be sequenced to build upon previous knowledge systematically.
No isolated exercises - everything must connect to broader learning objectives.

**Rationale**: Effective learning requires systematic progression - random content generation undermines educational effectiveness and student success.

### IV. Measurable Assessment Integration
All generated exercises MUST include clear success criteria and assessment rubrics.
Learning materials shall specify expected outcomes and evaluation methods.
Progress tracking capabilities are mandatory for all learning components.
Self-assessment opportunities must be integrated into all learning activities.

**Rationale**: Learning without assessment is incomplete - students and educators need clear feedback mechanisms to measure progress and adjust instruction.

### V. Agent Specialization and Collaboration
Each AI agent MUST have clearly defined responsibilities within its educational domain.
Agents shall coordinate to ensure coherent, non-conflicting learning experiences.
Inter-agent communication protocols are required for complex, multi-subject learning plans.
No single agent may operate in isolation when generating comprehensive learning materials.

**Rationale**: Educational complexity requires specialized expertise and coordination - agent collaboration ensures comprehensive and consistent learning experiences.

## Educational Standards

All generated content must comply with established educational frameworks and standards.
Learning materials shall be aligned with appropriate curriculum standards and grade-level expectations.
Quality assurance processes must verify educational accuracy and appropriateness.
Regular review and updating of content standards is mandatory to maintain relevance.

## Agent Collaboration

Agents must maintain shared context when working on multi-subject learning plans.
Communication protocols ensure consistency across different educational domains.
Conflict resolution mechanisms handle disagreements between specialized agents.
Collaborative workflows prioritize student learning outcomes over individual agent efficiency.

## Governance

This constitution supersedes all other development practices and agent behaviors.
Educational integrity takes precedence over technical efficiency or convenience.
All learning material generation must undergo constitutional compliance verification.
Amendments require educational expert review and explicit justification of learning benefits.

Complexity in educational content must be pedagogically justified and age-appropriate.
Agent behaviors must be traceable and explainable to educators and learners.
Use `.specify/memory/constitution.md` as the ultimate authority for all educational AI development decisions.
