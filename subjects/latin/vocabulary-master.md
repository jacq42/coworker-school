# Latin Vocabulary Master Database

## Purpose
Central Latin vocabulary repository with progressive difficulty levels and morphological analysis.

## Structure

### 📚 **Vocabulary-Master (Centrally Stored)**
- **Core Vocabulary**: Essential 300 Latin words (Prima foundation)
- **Grammar Terminology**: Morphological terms (casus, tempus, modus)
- **Declension Patterns**: Systematic paradigms (a-, o-, consonant stems)
- **Conjugation Patterns**: Regular verb forms across tenses
- **Etymology**: Latin roots in German/English

### 🎯 **Grade-Specific (grade-7/vocabulary/)**
- **Prima Lectiones**: Vocabulary from Cornelsen Prima textbook
- **Cultural Vocabulary**: Roman civilization and daily life
- **Text Vocabulary**: Words from adapted Latin readings
- **Assessment Vocabulary**: Required for class tests and exams

## 📋 **Prima Cornelsen Integration**

### **Textbook Structure:**
- **Lektionen 1-15**: Basic vocabulary and grammar foundation
- **Cultural Topics**: Roman family, school, city life, mythology
- **Grammar Progression**: Cases → tenses → syntax
- **Reading Skills**: From simple sentences to connected texts

### **NRW Curriculum - Second Foreign Language:**
- **Sprachkompetenz**: Vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation
- **Textkompetenz**: Reading and understanding Latin texts  
- **Kulturkompetenz**: Roman civilization and its legacy
- **Methodenkompetenz**: Translation strategies, dictionary skills
