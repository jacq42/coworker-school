## Nova Vocabula (Librum p. 15-18)

### Domus (House - fem., 4th decl.)
- **villa, -ae (f.)** - country house, villa
- **atrium, -i (n.)** - central hall  
- **cubiculum, -i (n.)** - bedroom
- **culina, -ae (f.)** - kitchen
- **hortus, -i (m.)** - garden

### Familia (Family)
- **dominus, -i (m.)** - master of house
- **domina, -ae (f.)** - lady of house  
- **servus, -i (m.)** - male slave
- **ancilla, -ae (f.)** - female slave

### Descriptive Adjectives
- **magnus, -a, -um** - large, great
- **parvus, -a, -um** - small
- **pulcher, -chra, -chrum** - beautiful

## Exempla Sententiarum (Example Sentences)
"Villa magna et pulchra est." (The villa is large and beautiful.)
"Dominus in atrio stat." (The master stands in the atrium.)
"Ancilla in culina laborat." (The slave girl works in the kitchen.)
