# English Vocabulary Master Database

## Purpose
Central vocabulary repository with progressive difficulty levels and cross-references to grade-specific applications.

## Structure

### 📚 **Vocabulary-Master (Centrally Stored)**
- **Core Vocabulary**: High-frequency words for A2 level (800-1000 words)
- **Grammar Terminology**: Basic grammatical terms (noun, verb, tense, etc.)
- **Function Words**: Articles, prepositions, connectors
- **Academic Language**: Basic school-related terminology
- **Common Patterns**: Phrasal verbs, collocations, idioms

### 🎯 **Grade-Specific (grade-7/vocabulary/)**
- **Textbook Vocabulary**: Unit-based words from A2 curriculum
- **Thematic Vocabulary**: Topics appropriate for 13-year-olds
- **Communicative Functions**: Language for real-life situations
- **Assessment Vocabulary**: Words required for A2 certification

## 📋 **A2 Level Specifications (CEFR)**

### **Vocabulary Requirements:**
- **Total Vocabulary**: ~1000 active words
- **Topics**: Personal information, family, shopping, local geography, employment
- **Grammar**: Present/past tenses, future plans, modal verbs
- **Functions**: Describing, comparing, expressing opinions

### **NRW Curriculum Integration:**
- **Kommunikative Kompetenzen**: Listening, speaking, reading, writing
- **Interkulturelle Kompetenzen**: UK/US cultural awareness
- **Methodische Kompetenzen**: Learning strategies, self-assessment

## 📋 **Practical Implementation**

### **Example Distribution:**

**Vocabulary-Master:**
- "analyze", "evaluate", "compare" (Academic verbs)
- "noun", "adjective", "preposition" (Grammar terms)
- "first", "second", "third" (Universal sequencing)

**Grade-7/Vocabulary:**
- Unit-specific words from current textbook
- "My favorite hobby is skateboarding" (Unit 3 - Hobbies)
- "The library is next to the gym" (Unit 1 - School)

