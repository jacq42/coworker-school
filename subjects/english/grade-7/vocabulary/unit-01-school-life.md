# Unit 1: School Life

## Learning Objectives
- Master 20 school-related vocabulary items
- Use in present tense sentences
- Describe school routines and facilities

## New Vocabulary (Textbook pp. 8-12)

### School Places
- **classroom** - where lessons happen
- **library** - for reading and studying
- **gymnasium** - for PE classes  
- **cafeteria** - for lunch
- **principal's office** - administration

### School Subjects
- **mathematics/math** - numbers and equations
- **science** - biology, chemistry, physics
- **history** - about the past
- **English** - language arts
- **art** - creative drawing/painting

### Daily Activities
- **study** - prepare for tests
- **homework** - work done at home
- **assignment** - task given by teacher
- **quiz** - short test
- **project** - longer assignment

## Example Sentences (From Curriculum)
"The library is next to the gym."
"We have math class in room 205."
"My favorite subject is art."
